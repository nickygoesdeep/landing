Who it’s for

Designed for bettors who care about process, not just picks.

SlipTrack is built for NHL-focused users who place multiple bet types and want a structured way to track decisions and outcomes.

Daily NHL Bettors

  • Track active slips without switching between apps.
  • Understand live status of team and player prop legs.
  • Keep results organized by ticket and date.

Parlay / SGP Builders

  • Create multi-leg entries with structured leg detail.
  • See odds context and payout preview more clearly.
  • Avoid losing track of long-leg combinations.

Data-Minded Sports Fans

  • Use bet history to identify strengths and weak spots.
  • Review outcomes in a consistent, less noisy UI.
  • Build better discipline with complete tracking.

Mobile-First Users

  • Built for fast interactions on phone first.
  • Smooth, scroll-friendly UI that scales to desktop.
  • PWA mindset for app-like usage patterns.
